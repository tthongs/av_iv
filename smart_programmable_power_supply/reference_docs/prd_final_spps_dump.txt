=== PARAGRAPH 83 [Heading 1] ===
Product Overview

=== PARAGRAPH 84 [Heading 2] ===
Product - Business Perspective

=== PARAGRAPH 85 [normal] ===
The Smart Programmable Voltage Supply Module is an intelligent, high-density benchtop and automated test equipment (ATE) power supply delivering a tightly regulated, programmable DC voltage output from 5.0 V to 20.0 V at currents up to 3.0 A (60 W continuous output power) from universal grid AC mains input (85 V to 265 V AC RMS, 47 Hz to 63 Hz).

=== PARAGRAPH 86 [normal] ===
Traditional bench power supplies rely on bulky 50 Hz iron-core transformers, inefficient linear regulators, and manual dials, which lead to high thermal dissipation, large footprint, and lack of automated telemetry. This product eliminates those limitations by integrating a universal mains (QR) Flyback converter, an ultra-efficient 4-switch synchronous Buck-Boost post-regulator, real-time dual-domain power telemetry, and autonomous analog hardware protection into a single compact board.

=== PARAGRAPH 87 [normal] ===
All requirements and functional parameters in this document are captured from the perspective of high-efficiency benchtop prototyping and automated modular test racks.

=== PARAGRAPH 88 [Heading 2] ===
Product – Technical Perspective

=== PARAGRAPH 89 [normal] ===
The Smart Programmable Power Supply is a 4-stage AC-to-DC conversion and regulation system in which the power stage and mixed-signal microcontroller work together to deliver precise, clean, and safe DC power. The module connects directly to universal grid AC mains (85V–265V AC) through an input protection and EMI filter stage, rectifies it to a high-voltage bulk DC rail (+120V to +375V DC), isolates and steps it down via a Flyback stage to an intermediate +24.0V DC bus (72W), and regulates it through a synchronous 4-switch Buck-Boost stage to provide the commanded 5.0V to 20.0V DC output at up to 3.0A.

The onboard microcontroller (STM32G474) acts as the system brain by reading dual-domain electrical telemetry (primary AC input power and secondary DC load power), computing real-time conversion efficiency, updating the 1.3-inch OLED dashboard, and driving the precision 12-bit DAC voltage steering network. Circuit protection relies on robust, standard, industry-proven hardware protections: primary AC fuse, inrush NTC, surge MOV, primary cycle-by-cycle Flyback current limiting, and secondary LM5176 built-in cycle-by-cycle overcurrent protection with thermal shutdown.

In simple terms, the power conversion stages handle the heavy electrical energy flow across certified 3000V RMS galvanic isolation, while the built-in controller protections and MCU provide safety, precision regulation, and live telemetry.

The primary power supply functionality includes:
• Universal AC Grid Operation: Accepts 85V to 265V AC RMS (47–63 Hz) without manual line selector switches.
• Reinforced Galvanic Isolation: Certified 3000V AC RMS (1 min) isolation meeting IEC 62368-1 SELV.
• Intermediate Regulated Rail: Flyback converter generates a stable +24.0V DC bus (72W) with > 90% peak efficiency.
• Wide Programmable DC Output: 4-switch synchronous Buck-Boost post-regulator delivers 5.0V to 20.0V DC at up to 3.0A continuous (60W continuous power).
• High-Resolution DAC Voltage Steering: 12-bit DAC network achieves 10mV monotonic setpoint resolution.
• Built-in Cycle-by-Cycle Hardware Current Limiting: LM5176 monitors output current through a dedicated 10mΩ shunt across its CSP/CSN pins, natively limiting peak inductor current on every switching cycle without external analog complexity.
• Built-in Controller Protections: Integrated input fuse (2A), NTC inrush limiter (10Ω), MOV transient clamp (300V), LM5176 internal thermal shutdown (165°C), and Under-Voltage Lockout (UVLO).
• Real-Time Dual-Domain Telemetry: Measures AC input power and DC load power, computing live efficiency (η) at 10 Hz.
• Interactive Local Dashboard: 1.3" OLED display shows live V, I, P, and efficiency meters; dual rotary encoders allow intuitive tuning; front-panel LEDs indicate CV, Current Limit, and Fault states.
• Low Standby Power: Consumes < 150 mW in idle state when output is disabled.

=== PARAGRAPH 99 [Heading 2] ===
Use Cases

=== PARAGRAPH 100 [normal] ===
The primary power supply functionality includes four operating modes:
• Constant Voltage (CV) Mode: Regulates output voltage within ±0.2% across varying load currents below the current limit.
• Current-Limited (OCP) Mode: Restricts peak inductor current cycle-by-cycle via LM5176 CS/CSG sense during overload or short-circuit.
• MCU Supervisory Protection Mode: Safely disables switching via LM5176 ENABLE pin on persistent overload or over-temperature.
• Standby / Idle Mode: Output power stage disabled while +24V intermediate rail is maintained with standby power < 150mW.

=== PARAGRAPH 102 [normal] ===
The benchtop operator interacts with the power supply using front-panel dual rotary controls and a 1.3" graphical OLED display. Power is delivered to the device under test (DUT) via heavy-duty 4mm binding posts, and operational status is indicated via front-panel mode LEDs.

=== PARAGRAPH 192 [Heading 1] ===
Assumptions

=== PARAGRAPH 193 [normal] ===
The following points are considered as assumptions for further project execution:
• Grid AC mains is nominal sinusoidal waveform with Total Harmonic Distortion (THD) ≤ 5%.
• Operating ambient temperature is -20°C to +50°C under natural convection airflow.
• Secondary DC ground is floating with reinforced galvanic isolation (≥ 3000V RMS) from Earth and AC mains.
• Connected prototype loads are predominantly resistive or capacitive (C_load ≤ 1000 µF).
